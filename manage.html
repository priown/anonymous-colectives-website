Manage the shop

A private helper page (not linked from the website). It changes nothing by itself: it writes the text you paste into js/products.js, and checks your photos.

1. Prices

  1. Type the old price (normal price) and the sale price for each product. Leave the sale price empty if it is not on sale.
  2. Press Copy price list.
  3. Open js/products.js, select everything from const PRICE_LIST = { to its closing };, paste, and save.
